From my kitchen 🍎

A Little Homemade Goodness

My kitchen has been busy lately, and I've been putting my pressure canner to work! This month, I stocked our pantry with homemade canned potatoes and decided to try my hand at making cinnamon applesauce from scratch.

Twenty-one pounds of apples later, I ended up with seven quarts of the most delicious, smooth cinnamon applesauce! And yes, I've already decided this recipe is a keeper. 😂

There's something so satisfying about turning simple ingredients into something homemade we can enjoy later. Between canning, cooking, and getting ready for holiday baking, my kitchen has definitely been getting a workout! 🍎🤎

Around the Lowcountry 🌿

A Little Christmas Magic on James Island 🎄

One of our favorite ways to kick off the Christmas season is enjoying the Holiday Festival of Lights at James Island County Park!

Between the twinkling lights, festive atmosphere, and roasting marshmallows by the fire, it's one of those outings that makes the season feel extra special. And of course, the boys are always happy when marshmallows are involved! 😂

If you're looking for a fun holiday activity around Charleston, this is definitely one to add to your Christmas bucket list. ❤️

Home sweet home 🏡

Heading Out of Town for the Holidays? 🎄

Between packing, wrapping presents, and remembering everything on your holiday to-do list, it's easy to forget about the house you're leaving behind!

Before heading out, unplug unnecessary decorations, double-check doors and windows, adjust your thermostat for the weather, and ask a trusted neighbor to keep an eye on things while you're away. A little preparation makes it easier to enjoy your trip. ❤️
